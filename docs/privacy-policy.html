Village Run: privacy policy

App: Village Run: Bharat Yatra (package com.sharadkushwaha.villagerun) · Developer: Sharad Singh Kushwaha · Last updated: 1 October 2026

This policy explains how the Village Run game for Android handles information. In short: the game does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, advertising ID or any other personal or sensitive information. There is no account or sign-in.

Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves your game progress on your own device only: laddoos, keys, taslas, best score, the runner and costumes you unlocked and chose, and your sound setting. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to the developer or anyone else. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes it. If you have turned on Android's own device backup, Android may include this game data in your personal Google backup; that backup is controlled by you and Google, not by the developer.

Advertising, analytics and purchases

The game contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting services and no third-party tracking software. It has no in-app purchases; laddoos and keys are earned only by playing and have no real-money value.

Internet and permissions

The game works fully offline and does not send data over the internet. It does not request access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage or other sensitive device features.

Children

Village Run is designed to be suitable for everyone, including children. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children. It complies with Google Play's Families policy.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any information, because we do not collect any.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page and shown inside the game, with a new "last updated" date.
