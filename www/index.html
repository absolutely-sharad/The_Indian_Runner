Privacy policy

Village Run is a game made by Sharad Singh Kushwaha. This policy explains what the game does with information. Last updated 1 October 2026.

We do not collect personal information. The game does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal details, and it has no account sign-in.

What stays on your device. Your laddoos, keys, taslas, best score, chosen runner, costumes and sound setting are saved only on this device, so you can continue where you left off. This data never leaves your device, and uninstalling the game deletes it.

No ads, no tracking, no in-app purchases. The game has no advertising, no analytics, no third-party tracking tools and no real-money purchases. Laddoos and keys are earned only by playing.

No internet needed. The game works fully offline and does not send data to any server.

Children. The game is suitable for all ages, including children. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Permissions. The game does not ask for any special device permissions such as camera, microphone, location or contacts.

Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the game and on the store listing.

Contact. Questions about privacy: sharadsingh0203@gmail.com
